# Demand validation contract

Two hypotheses are in scope.

## H1 — AI Delivery Control Kit

Builders will use a deterministic checklist/CLI to decide when to introduce a Gateway and to prevent infrastructure-first delay.

Valid demand evidence:

- a non-owner user runs the tool and shares the result;
- an external issue requests a real improvement;
- a project links the toolkit as part of its workflow;
- a maintainer contribution is accepted.

Stars alone are an attention signal, not verified use.

## H2 — AI Project Delivery Audit

An operator or founder will provide project evidence for a bounded audit that identifies delivery blockers, evidence substitutions, and the smallest next Work Package.

Valid demand evidence:

- a qualified person submits a real project problem;
- an audit output is delivered and explicitly accepted;
- payment is recorded for a paid audit.

## Revenue rule

Revenue remains `0 VERIFIED` until a transaction record can be reconciled to an offer, payer, amount, and delivery obligation. Intent, sign-up, invoice, or verbal agreement is not transaction evidence.
